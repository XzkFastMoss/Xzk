# FastMoss product and copy reference

Use the following product language consistently. Do not invent market-leadership claims, performance guarantees, customer numbers, prices, or discounts.

## Product definition

Primary Chinese definition:

> FastMoss 是 TikTok 电商数据分析与增长决策平台。

Expanded explanation:

> 帮助 TikTok 商家、品牌和服务商看清市场、竞品、达人与内容，发现增长机会，更快做出经营决策。

Short English definition:

> TikTok e-commerce data intelligence and growth decision platform.

## Core capabilities

Use these official functional labels without renaming them:

1. **数据分析** — 看市场趋势、类目增长、店铺与商品表现。
2. **竞对调研** — 拆解竞品店铺、商品、达人与内容打法。
3. **发掘达人** — 找到与类目和商品匹配、具备带货潜力的达人。
4. **内容洞察** — 发现热视频、素材趋势、受众反馈与爆款方向。

For small canvases, show the four names plus one selected explanation. For feature education or motion, explain all four.

## Data proof points

Preserve the values, plus signs, and labels exactly unless updated source material is supplied:

- `380万+ 全球用户`
- `5亿+ 店铺与商品数据`
- `3亿+ 达人数据`
- `1200天+ 持续积累`

Do not change `持续积累` to `历史数据` when the exact campaign label is required. Do not combine values into an unsupported aggregate.

## MCP · CLI · Agent

Primary launch message:

> MCP · CLI · Agent 全面开放

Product explanation:

> 让 FastMoss 数据进入商家的工作流，通过自然语言完成跨市场、竞品、达人和内容的联动分析。

Example prompt:

> 分析美国美妆市场，找出增长类目、头部竞品、潜力达人和热门内容。

Example result labels:

- 增长类目
- 竞品打法
- 潜力达人
- 内容趋势

Avoid describing MCP, CLI, or Agent as autonomous business decision-makers. Position them as access and workflow capabilities that support analysis and decisions.

## Anniversary and member month

Approved light-marketing forms:

- `FastMoss 四周年会员月`
- `四年持续积累，让每一次增长判断更有依据`
- `与全球 TikTok 商家一起，看见更多增长可能`

Do not add `年付低至5折`, `月付39元起`, countdowns, or scarcity language unless the user explicitly requests current offer communication and the claim is verified.

## Brand lines

- `从看见数据，到看懂增长`
- `让每一次增长判断，都更接近答案`
- `更快找到方向，更稳做出决策`
- `做 TikTok，就用 FastMoss`

Use at most one primary brand line per static KV. A second may be used only as the closing line in motion.

## Copy hierarchy recipes

### Product awareness

- Eyebrow: `FastMoss 是什么？`
- Headline: `TikTok 电商数据分析与增长决策平台`
- Support: `看市场 · 查竞品 · 找达人 · 懂内容`
- CTA: `做 TikTok，就用 FastMoss`

### Feature campaign

- Eyebrow: `一个平台，看懂 TikTok 电商生意`
- Headline: select one business outcome, such as `找到增长机会之前，先看清市场全景`
- Proof: four feature labels or a relevant product crop.
- CTA: campaign-specific or `立即了解 FastMoss`.

### AI workflow

- Eyebrow: `不止能看，还能直接调用`
- Headline: `MCP · CLI · Agent 全面开放`
- Support: `一句话，联动市场、竞品、达人与内容数据`
- CTA: `用 FastMoss，让数据进入工作流`

### Data proof

- Eyebrow: `每一次判断，都有数据支撑`
- Headline: `看见更完整的 TikTok 电商全景`
- Proof: display the four approved data labels.
- CTA: `从数据出发，找到增长方向`.

## Writing rules

- Lead with user value, then capability, then proof.
- Keep Chinese headlines concrete and short. Prefer verbs such as 看、拆、找、发现、分析、判断.
- Use `竞对调研`, not vague forms such as `智能洞察全域竞对生态`.
- Avoid empty claims: 赋能、颠覆、遥遥领先、全能、万能、秒出爆品、保证增长.
- Avoid implying official TikTok affiliation or endorsement.
- For new audiences, never lead only with `MCP · CLI · Agent`; first establish what FastMoss is or connect the terms directly to business data.
