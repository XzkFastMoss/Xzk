# Market material recipes

The dimensions below are production defaults, not permanent platform rules. Verify current delivery specifications when a platform or media buyer provides them.

## Xiaohongshu cover / feed image

- Default canvas: 1242 × 1660, 3:4.
- Use a white/warm-white base with pink decoration; do not switch to a dark cover for attention.
- Keep logo and headline within the upper two-thirds; reserve lower space for feed overlays and crop tolerance.
- Use 8–18 Chinese characters for the main cover headline when possible.
- Make one concept readable at thumbnail scale.
- Avoid dense feature matrices. Prefer product definition plus one benefit or one four-item feature block.
- For account variants, change the editorial hook, composition, crop, and proof order—not only color.

## Douyin / WeChat Channels / vertical social video

- Default canvas: 1080 × 1920, 9:16, 30 fps.
- Keep the background white or warm white. Use pink/purple shapes, grids, light fields, and assets for motion contrast instead of dark scene changes.
- Keep critical content inside approximately 72 px side margins and away from platform overlays.
- First 2 seconds: define the problem or product category.
- Recommended 20–30 second structure:
  1. What FastMoss is.
  2. Four capabilities.
  3. Data proof.
  4. MCP/CLI/Agent or campaign message.
  5. Brand close.
- Use the exact logo as a persistent overlay only when the brief requests it; otherwise keep a consistent but less intrusive brand anchor.
- Design for silent viewing. Add captions for all product meaning.

## Social static ad

- Common canvases: 1080 × 1080, 1200 × 1500, or buyer-specified dimensions.
- Use one headline, one visual, one proof block, one CTA.
- Use a light product canvas; place saturated brand colors only in the focal object, text emphasis, CTA, or edge rule.
- Prioritize product meaning over decorative campaign language.
- Do not place all four features and all four data labels on a square unless the type remains legible at feed size.

## Web hero

- Default design canvas: 1920 × 800–960; implement responsively.
- Use left-copy/right-visual or centered-copy/oversized-background-visual layouts.
- Include product definition above the fold for acquisition traffic.
- Keep the full-bleed hero light: white/warm-white base, pink decorative light, slate copy, and one product-led focal point.
- Preserve live HTML text and SVG logo. Use supplied KV assets as optimized image layers.
- Ensure the mobile crop retains the logo, headline, CTA, and one product/hero cue.

## Event screen / exhibition board

- Use organizer-provided dimensions and safe areas.
- Increase logo and headline scale; reduce small body text.
- Prefer one hero asset plus large data proof or product definition.
- Test from the expected viewing distance, not only on a desktop screen.

## Sales visual / presentation cover

- Default canvas: 16:9.
- Cover: product definition plus one campaign line; no feature grid unless it is the subject.
- Section divider: use one capability and a relevant asset crop.
- Keep all elements editable and aligned to a shared grid.

## Email / CRM banner

- Default design canvas: 1200 × 480–600; export a 2× version if the email platform supports responsive sizing.
- Keep the headline short and high-contrast; do not embed all campaign detail in the image.
- Use one CTA in live email HTML when possible rather than only inside the image.

## Creative territory recipes

### Data Intelligence KV

- Mode: white product editorial.
- Hero: globe or data visual.
- Headline: product definition or data-led benefit.
- Proof: one to four approved data labels.
- Motion: slow pale-pink grid drift, number count-up, chart/data-node reveal.

### Competitor Research KV

- Mode: white product editorial with comparison lines and one pink active state.
- Hero: comparison columns, magnified product/store crop, directional markers.
- Copy: emphasize studying stores, products, creators, and content strategy.
- Avoid using third-party logos unless supplied and authorized.

### Creator Discovery KV

- Mode: white product editorial with a soft rose light field.
- Hero: creator network/nodes or authorized creator imagery.
- Copy: emphasize category fit and selling potential, not celebrity.
- Never use real creators' portraits without authorization.

### Content Insight KV

- Mode: white product editorial with pink active states.
- Hero: vertical content frames, trend line, content tags.
- Copy: emphasize hot videos, creative trends, audience response, and breakout direction.
- Avoid reproducing copyrighted third-party videos as hero content.

### AI Workflow KV

- Mode: soft AI white.
- Hero: Agent prompt/interface plus four connected result modules.
- Headline: `MCP · CLI · Agent 全面开放`.
- Support: explicitly connect the capability to market, competitor, creator, and content data.

### Anniversary Member Month KV

- Mode: white celebration.
- Hero: `hero-kv-four.webp`, optionally supported by ribbon.
- Brand message: four years of data accumulation and merchant companionship.
- Keep discount/pricing secondary or omit it for brand-first social materials.
