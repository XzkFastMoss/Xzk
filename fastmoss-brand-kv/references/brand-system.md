# FastMoss visual system

These production rules are derived from the supplied FastMoss website, campaign assets, and the approved V3 product-function video. If an official corporate brand book is later provided, it overrides this reference.

## Brand idea

Express FastMoss as an intelligent TikTok e-commerce operating system: data-rich, decisive, fast, global, and AI-enabled. Use a bright product-led editorial language—clean white space, precise information, and confident pink accents—rather than a generic dark SaaS or cyberpunk style.

## Non-negotiable background rule

- Use `#FFFFFF`, `#FFFDFC`, or a very pale pink-tinted white as the full-canvas background.
- Do not use `#10111A`, black, charcoal, dark navy, or a dark gradient as the full-canvas background.
- Do not use a full-canvas saturated pink or purple gradient. Keep saturated color in decorations, hero assets, CTA elements, charts, active states, or a small local emphasis panel.
- Let white space occupy the majority of the canvas. Aim for a visual impression close to the V3 feature scenes: white product surface, pink decorative glow, dark slate typography, and restrained purple accents.
- A `#1F283C` slate panel may be used locally for one active feature, one prompt, or one high-contrast proof block. Keep it subordinate and avoid letting dark areas dominate the page.

## Logo

- Use `assets/fastmoss-logo.svg` unchanged.
- Preserve aspect ratio; never stretch, skew, rotate, recolor, outline, redraw, or add effects to the logo.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least half the height of the circular planet mark.
- Minimum practical digital width: 122 px at 1×. Use a larger size on mobile-first covers.
- On white or warm-white backgrounds, place the logo directly on the canvas or in a subtle white rounded field with a restrained shadow.
- If the logo overlaps a pink/purple decorative area or image, add a clean white field behind it.
- Keep the logo away from platform UI overlays and cropping zones.

## Core palette

| Role | Color | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| FastMoss pink | `#FE2062` | Primary brand signal, headline emphasis, CTA, active state |
| Bright rose | `#FF6D9E` | Supporting highlight, soft light, gradient bridge |
| Brand purple | `#7B3FF2` | Depth, AI/data association, secondary highlight |
| Gradient purple | `#6C36D9` | Decorative gradient endpoint |
| Slate | `#1F283C` | Primary text and small local high-contrast panels |
| Paper | `#FFFDFC` | Default warm-white background |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Default clean product background and cards |
| Soft rose | `#FFF3F7` | Decorative background tint and section wash |
| Border | `#EDE7EC` | Dividers, card borders, product structure |
| Secondary text | `#6A7383` | Supporting copy on light surfaces |

Preferred decorative treatments:

- Brand emphasis: `linear-gradient(100deg, #FE2062, #7B3FF2)` for text accents, CTA, edge rules, or compact graphic elements.
- Soft pink wash: radial pink at 8–14% opacity fading to transparent on white.
- Soft purple wash: radial purple at 4–9% opacity fading to transparent, used as secondary depth.
- Product-card shadow: broad slate or pink shadow at 5–10% opacity; keep edges clean.

Do not use rainbow gradients or introduce a competing saturated blue/green palette. Green is acceptable only for small positive-status indicators.

## Typography

- Chinese: `PingFang SC`, `Noto Sans SC`, or `Microsoft YaHei` in that order when available.
- Latin and numerals: use the same sans-serif family; `JetBrains Mono` may be used sparingly for indices, prompts, code, or telemetry.
- Headlines: 800–900 weight, compact line height around 1.05–1.15, slight negative tracking at large sizes.
- Body: 550–700 weight, line height around 1.35–1.55.
- Set primary copy in `#1F283C`; use FastMoss pink or the pink-purple gradient only for the key word or phrase.
- Avoid ultralight type, condensed novelty fonts, faux italics, and decorative Chinese fonts.
- Use no more than three font sizes and two weight families in one static KV unless a data table requires more.

## Graphic language

- Prefer asymmetric editorial layouts, oversized cropped hero elements, structured feature rows, thin data lines, soft pink glows, and generous white space.
- Use 54–72 px grid modules for large digital canvases; reduce proportionally for smaller formats.
- Use white rounded rectangles for interface/data containers. Typical radius: 24–40 px on a 1080-wide canvas.
- Use thin slate/pink borders at 6–10% opacity. Shadows should be soft and spatial, not heavy black drop shadows.
- Add a thin pink-to-purple rule at one edge of the canvas when a stronger brand finish is needed, as in the V3 product-function reference.
- Use the supplied ribbon, globe, data, AI, and anniversary-4 assets as hero or secondary imagery; avoid showing all of them at once.
- One canvas should have one dominant visual mass. Secondary data cards support it rather than compete with it.

## Layout modes

### White product editorial — default

- Background: `#FFFFFF` or `#FFFDFC`.
- Type: `#1F283C` with pink/purple emphasis.
- Decoration: pale pink radial light, fine grid, small index numbers, and a thin gradient edge rule.
- Visual: structured feature rows, product screenshots, charts, comparison blocks.
- Best for product definition, education, sales enablement, web heroes, and social covers.

### Soft AI white

- Background: white or warm white.
- Type: slate with pink headline emphasis.
- Decoration: a soft rose-purple light field behind `kv-ai.webp` or the Agent interface; keep the rest of the canvas white.
- Visual: Agent prompt/interface plus up to four connected result modules.
- Best for MCP, CLI, Agent, AI workflow, and product-launch communication.

### White celebration

- Background: white or soft rose-white.
- Type: slate with pink emphasis.
- Decoration: cropped anniversary `4`, ribbon, pale pink glow, and small campaign badge.
- Visual: one celebratory hero asset; keep the campaign message product-led.
- Best for member month, anniversary, launches, and event communications.

## Safe composition

- Reserve 6–8% of canvas width as the base outer margin.
- For 1080 × 1920 video, start with 64–72 px left/right margins and keep critical text outside top/bottom platform overlay zones.
- For 3:4 feed covers, place the headline and brand anchor in the central safe area so automated crops retain meaning.
- Test every design as a thumbnail. The logo, headline, and one primary visual must remain identifiable.

## Prohibited treatments

- Do not use `#10111A` or any dark full-canvas background.
- Do not create a black, navy, charcoal, neon-cyberpunk, or dark terminal aesthetic.
- Do not redraw or generate the logo.
- Do not use fake product screenshots or fabricated charts presented as real data.
- Do not overwhelm a KV with every product function and every campaign claim.
- Do not use price, discount, urgency, or scarcity as the dominant brand device unless explicitly requested.
- Do not use unrelated stock e-commerce imagery, generic neon technology imagery, or excessive 3D glass cards.
- Do not place long paragraphs into a hero visual.
