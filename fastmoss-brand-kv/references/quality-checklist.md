# FastMoss market-material quality checklist

Run every applicable section before delivery.

## Product clarity

- Can a new customer identify FastMoss as a TikTok e-commerce data analysis and growth decision platform without reading the post caption?
- Is at least one real business outcome clear?
- If MCP/CLI/Agent appears, is it connected to market, competitor, creator, or content data?
- Does the visual avoid reducing FastMoss to an anniversary event or a discount offer?

## Brand integrity

- Is the exact SVG logo used without distortion, recoloring, redraw, or effects?
- Does the logo have sufficient clear space and contrast?
- Is the full-canvas background white, warm white, or a very pale pink-tinted white?
- Is `#10111A` absent as a background color, with no black, charcoal, dark navy, or dark full-canvas gradient replacing it?
- Does the palette stay within FastMoss pink, purple, slate, paper, white, soft rose, and light neutral borders?
- Are pink and purple used as decoration or emphasis while white space remains dominant?
- Does the design use one dominant visual rather than a collection of unrelated cards?
- Are supplied KV assets used in a way that matches their purpose?

## Copy and claims

- Are `数据分析`, `竞对调研`, `发掘达人`, and `内容洞察` spelled consistently?
- If shown, do the data labels exactly read `380万+ 全球用户`, `5亿+ 店铺与商品数据`, `3亿+ 达人数据`, and `1200天+ 持续积累`?
- Are price, discount, urgency, ranking, growth, and performance claims explicitly supplied and verified?
- Does the copy avoid empty superlatives and implied TikTok endorsement?
- Are punctuation, spacing, capitalization, and Chinese/English mixing consistent?

## Composition and accessibility

- Can the poster's single creative idea be summarized in one sentence?
- Is there one dominant visual center rather than evenly weighted competing elements?
- Do the headline and hero visual explain the same business idea?
- Does the composition establish three levels: first-glance hook, second-glance proof, and third-glance action/details?
- Does every major decorative element carry a product, campaign, or business meaning?
- Is the CTA placed at the natural end of the reading path?
- Is the reading order obvious at first glance?
- Is the headline readable at feed-thumbnail size?
- Are body copy and numbers above the practical minimum size for the delivery format?
- Are critical elements inside platform-safe areas and crop zones?
- Is text contrast strong enough on every background?
- Are screenshots large enough to communicate the intended feature?
- If a reference poster was supplied, were only its composition and creative mechanisms learned while its palette, identity, and recognizable trade dress were not copied?

## Asset and rights safety

- Are all third-party portraits, logos, screenshots, video clips, and music authorized?
- Are there no third-party watermarks or copied creative treatments?
- Are generated visuals used only for supporting art and not to fake product data or redraw the logo?

## Output QA

- Was the artifact rendered/exported at exact dimensions, aspect ratio, color mode, and requested file type?
- Was the final export visually inspected rather than only code-checked?
- For motion, were the opening, each transition, each key message, and the final frame inspected?
- For editable files, do text, logo, hero image, data proof, and CTA remain separate layers/components?
- Are final file paths, dimensions, duration/version, and assumptions included in the handoff?

## Multi-channel originality

- Do variants differ materially in hook, headline, composition, asset crop, proof order, and motion rhythm?
- Do they preserve the same approved brand facts and logo geometry?
- Is each variant tailored to the account's audience rather than disguised as a technical duplicate?
- Avoid claiming guaranteed platform-originality or review approval.
