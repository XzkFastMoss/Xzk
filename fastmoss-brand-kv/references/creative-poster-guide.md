# FastMoss creative poster guide

Use this guide to turn a marketing brief or reference poster into an original FastMoss concept. Learn composition, hierarchy, interaction, and conversion logic from references; do not copy their palette, brand identity, typography, recognizable graphic treatment, or trade dress. Apply the FastMoss light pink-purple visual system from `brand-system.md`.

## Creative standard

A creative market poster must do three things:

1. Attract attention with one dominant visual hook.
2. Explain a concrete product or campaign value within three seconds.
3. Lead the audience to one clear next action.

Use this concept formula:

`真实问题 + 视觉隐喻 + 明确观点 + 产品证据 + 行动入口`

- **真实问题:** name a decision, uncertainty, or tension the merchant recognizes.
- **视觉隐喻:** make the problem or solution visible through one meaningful composition.
- **明确观点:** express why the message matters; do not use a plain feature name as the headline.
- **产品证据:** show an authorized interface, approved data label, result structure, or workflow.
- **行动入口:** end with one CTA, QR code, URL, or campaign action.

## Reusable composition mechanisms

### Use one dominant visual center

Choose one hero and make it clearly larger than all supporting elements. Valid FastMoss heroes include:

- an oversized anniversary `4`;
- the AI cube;
- an Agent interface crop;
- one enlarged chart or analysis module;
- one approved data number;
- one natural-language prompt and its result;
- one creator, product, or competitor analysis view.

Other assets may support the hero, but must not carry equal visual weight. Avoid presenting the anniversary asset, AI cube, full screenshot, four features, four data labels, and CTA as six competing focal points.

### Prefer asymmetric balance

Create tension and editorial energy instead of centering every element.

- Pair a heavy left headline with a right-side product interface.
- Pair a large left number with a compact right explanation.
- Place a cropped anniversary `4` on one edge and an AI/product cue on the opposite side.
- Use type, data, whitespace, or a secondary object to balance the hero rather than mirroring it.
- Crop hero assets intentionally at the canvas edge when the meaning remains clear.

Asymmetry must still feel stable. Balance visual mass, contrast, and empty space rather than matching coordinates.

### Frame ordinary assets with a meaningful device

Turn a standard screenshot or object into a campaign visual by designing a concept-specific frame:

- pink-purple data tracks for market change;
- a pale perspective grid for data space;
- orbiting data lines around the AI cube;
- connected nodes around an Agent interface;
- four paths from market, competitor, creator, and content data into one workflow;
- an oversized `4` acting as a frame for an interface crop;
- white rounded data windows used as a structured product container.

The frame must communicate the campaign idea. Omit any decoration that could be replaced by an unrelated shape without changing the meaning.

### Use a headline block as a narrative turn

Let the hero show what is happening, then use one bold viewpoint to explain why it matters. A useful headline is a conclusion, tension, or change—not merely a feature label.

Prefer:

- `让数据不只被看见，还能直接进入工作流`
- `以前反复查的数据，现在一句话联动分析`
- `找达人，不只是看粉丝量`
- `对手的增长打法，藏在数据变化里`
- `四年持续积累，让每一次增长判断更有依据`

Avoid leading only with `Agent 上线`, `竞品分析`, `达人库`, or `数据平台`. Use these as explanation beneath the viewpoint.

### Ask a question to create psychological interaction

A concrete question makes the viewer participate before receiving the answer. Suitable FastMoss questions include:

- `如果只能选一个增长市场，你会怎么判断？`
- `同类商品都在增长，为什么你的没有？`
- `找达人时，你还只看粉丝量吗？`
- `一个竞品突然爆单，你会先查商品、达人还是内容？`
- `如果一句话就能分析市场、竞品、达人和内容，你会先问什么？`
- `这个类目是真增长，还是短期流量？`

Immediately connect the question to a FastMoss analysis action and result. Do not leave a decorative mystery with no product answer.

### Show evidence instead of abstract claims

Replace claims such as `强大`, `智能`, `全面洞察`, or `提升效率` with visible proof:

- a real Agent interface;
- a realistic natural-language prompt using approved product functions;
- structured results such as growth category, competitor strategy, creator recommendation, and content opportunity;
- approved proof points: `380万+ 全球用户`, `5亿+ 店铺与商品数据`, `3亿+ 达人数据`, `1200天+ 持续积累`;
- a concrete workflow or before/after operating model;
- a purposeful crop of an authorized product screenshot.

Never fabricate results or present decorative charts as real product data.

### Use one large number as a memory anchor

Numbers scan quickly on mobile. Select one number that owns the composition:

- `4` for the anniversary;
- `5亿+` for shop and product data;
- `3亿+` for creator data;
- `380万+` for global users;
- `1200天+` for continued accumulation.

Keep other numbers subordinate. Do not enlarge all four proof points simultaneously.

### Convert secondary information into tags

Use three or four compact tags for fast classification:

- core functions: `数据分析`, `竞对调研`, `发掘达人`, `内容洞察`;
- Agent results: `增长类目`, `竞品打法`, `潜力达人`, `内容趋势`;
- merchant tasks: `选市场`, `找商品`, `查竞品`, `找达人`, `看内容`.

Tags support the headline; they are not the headline. Keep them readable at feed-thumbnail size.

### Give decoration a concept

Use decoration only when its meaning is defensible:

- data tracks = changing markets;
- connecting lines = MCP/CLI/Agent entering a workflow;
- creator/content nodes = relationship discovery;
- magnified area = competitor research;
- four-way convergence = linked market, competitor, creator, and content analysis;
- data stream entering a prompt = natural-language access to FastMoss data;
- globe or coordinates = global TikTok e-commerce coverage;
- anniversary `4` = four years of accumulation and companionship.

Avoid generic neon technology, unrelated crowns, random glass shapes, or ornamental lines that do not support product meaning.

## Information hierarchy

Build three levels instead of giving every item equal weight.

### Level 1: first three seconds

- exact FastMoss logo;
- primary headline;
- one hero visual.

### Level 2: after the viewer pauses

- product explanation;
- one selected capability;
- one proof group, result structure, or short workflow.

### Level 3: after interest is established

- product definition;
- date, location, or campaign detail;
- CTA, QR code, or URL;
- legal or explanatory note.

Guide the eye through:

`Logo → headline → hero/product proof → supporting result → CTA`

Place the CTA at the natural end of this path. Keep QR codes adjacent to the CTA, inside platform-safe areas, surrounded by quiet space, and test them after export.

## FastMoss poster structures

### Question-driven poster

Best for Agent, competitor research, creator discovery, content insight, and product education.

1. FastMoss logo.
2. One real merchant question.
3. Enlarged interface or AI KV.
4. One viewpoint that answers the question.
5. Three or four result tags.
6. Product definition.
7. CTA.

Example narrative:

`竞品突然爆单，发生了什么？ → 一句话联动市场、竞品、达人与内容数据 → 增长类目 / 竞品打法 / 潜力达人 / 内容趋势 → 让数据进入工作流`

### Large-number proof poster

Best for anniversary, brand strength, data accumulation, and member month.

1. Logo.
2. One oversized number.
3. The number's exact meaning.
4. One brand viewpoint.
5. One product capability or interface.
6. Core function row.
7. CTA.

For the anniversary, let the large `4` attract attention and let the product explanation establish what FastMoss does. Do not reduce the poster to a celebration or offer.

### Product-evidence poster

Best for web heroes, product launches, and MCP/CLI/Agent communication.

1. Product-category definition.
2. Benefit-led headline.
3. Large real product interface.
4. One natural-language query.
5. Three or four result modules.
6. Launch message.
7. Brand close.

For acquisition audiences, establish FastMoss as a TikTok e-commerce data analysis and growth decision platform before relying on technical terms.

### Contrast poster

Best for education and pain-point communication.

- Show the old state on one side: many pages, repeated searching, manual consolidation.
- Show the new state on the other: one instruction, connected FastMoss data, structured results.
- Use a data path, transition, or Agent connector between states.

Express a change in operating method; never add unsupported efficiency multipliers or guaranteed outcomes.

## Brand adaptation

Keep the approved FastMoss appearance regardless of the reference poster:

- Use white, warm white, or a very pale pink-tinted full-canvas background.
- Use FastMoss pink for action and emphasis, purple for AI/data depth, and slate for primary text.
- Use pale pink radial light, fine grids, translucent rose forms, and a thin pink-purple edge rule.
- Use the exact SVG logo unchanged.
- Use authorized FastMoss KV assets and real product screenshots.
- Preserve generous whitespace.

Do not copy a reference poster's black/red palette, logo placement as trade dress, distinctive typography, brand symbols, or recognizable composition pixel-for-pixel. Abstract the mechanism, then rebuild it through FastMoss assets and product meaning.

## Concept workflow

Before composing, write a one-line answer for each item:

1. **One thing:** What single idea does this poster communicate?
2. **Audience tension:** What does the merchant need to decide, discover, or understand?
3. **Hook:** What will stop the eye at thumbnail size?
4. **Metaphor:** How does the hero make the idea visible?
5. **Viewpoint:** What concrete claim or insight becomes the headline?
6. **Proof:** Which real interface, approved number, or result structure supports it?
7. **Hierarchy:** What belongs to levels 1, 2, and 3?
8. **Action:** What should the viewer do next?

Then sketch at least two materially different layouts before polishing. Change composition, reading order, hero crop, and proof placement—not only colors or decorations.

## Review questions

- Can the concept be summarized in one sentence?
- What is the single dominant visual center?
- Can a new customer understand what FastMoss does without the post caption?
- Do the headline and hero explain the same idea?
- Is there one real product or data proof?
- Does every major decoration have a business meaning?
- Is the reading path obvious?
- Is the CTA the endpoint rather than a floating element?
- At thumbnail size, are the logo, headline, and hero still identifiable?
- If the pink-purple palette were removed, would the concept and composition still be interesting?

## Anti-patterns

- Do not treat a function list as the creative concept.
- Do not use six equally large focal elements.
- Do not place all four core functions and all four proof points into a small static KV.
- Do not let an anniversary, price, discount, partner wall, or QR code overpower the product meaning.
- Do not decorate with generic technology shapes unrelated to the message.
- Do not use a beautiful AI object without showing what business data or workflow it represents.
- Do not place the CTA randomly; make it the conclusion of the reading path.
- Do not imitate a reference poster's palette or brand identity.
- Do not claim guaranteed growth, conversion, platform approval, or originality.
